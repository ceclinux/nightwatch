# Nightwatch v0.4 "Jingo": Liquid Glass redesign

Date: 2026-09-24
Status: built in v0.4.0 (plan docs/plans/2026-09-24-v0.4-jingo.md)
Owner: rsutcliffe
Design source: the owner's handover "Liquid Glass design handover" (24 September 2026, Affinity mock-ups: Tonight widget v2.1, Targets card timeline v1.0). This spec takes the handover as the visual target and records where Nightwatch departs from it.
Builds on: the v0.1, v0.2 and v0.3 specs. Behaviour not named here stays as those specs say.

## 1. Purpose

Bring the popover and the Targets window into the macOS 26 and 27 Liquid Glass design language, and make both screens explain themselves: why the score is what it is, when each target is viewable inside the clear window, and what a warning means.

Success criteria:

1. On macOS 26 or later the popover panel and target cards render in the system Liquid Glass material (the tiles sit on the panel as a translucent surface.tile fill; see §7) (`glassEffect`), tinted dark, over the menu-bar popover; on macOS 14 and 15, and with Reduce Transparency on, the same layout renders on the solid fill with every other token unchanged.
2. The popover shows the sky score inside a 60-tick clock bezel whose ticks inside the clear window glow red, and the hourly cloud strip becomes clear-sky bars (fill height = 100 minus cloud).
3. The popover's reason line names what holds the score back ("Held back by a 97% moon and high dew risk") and is hidden when nothing does.
4. Every Targets card shows a viewability timeline across tonight's clear window: the viewable span lit in red with brightness following altitude, the best moment marked, and "Viewable 00:20–04:57" and "Best 03:30 · 81°" beneath.
5. Every text colour meets 4.5:1 on the glass in the worst case measured, and every chart has a sentence-form accessibility label.
6. The Command Line Tools build keeps working, with no Xcode project and no `@State`.

Status at v0.4.0:
- Criteria 1 and 2 are built, and wait on the owner's live look (UAT 19 and 20).
- Criterion 3 is met and tested.
- Criterion 4 is built and tested in SkyCore (viewable span, altitude samples, gradient stops).
- Criterion 5 is met: measured live on the panel and the tiles (§7), and on the solid fallback. Every chart has a sentence label.
- Criterion 6 is met.

## 2. Decisions taken with the owner

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Accent | Keep the app's red, #ff453a (`Theme.accent`). The handover's #EB5545 was only for visualising. Red is chosen to protect night vision at the telescope; the design notes say so, answering the handover's open question |
| EQ tilt | Stays in the popover header meta line, where 0.2.1 put it: "{date} · Bortle {n} · EQ tilt {lat}° true north". The handover's move to Settings is not taken |
| Name | Nightwatch. "Home Sky" in the handover came from the prototyping session guessing from a screenshot. Home is the site name in the eyebrow, from the active site |
| Patrol | The button stays "Patrol" (and "Refresh" in plain wording). It fetches the forecast now and recomputes everything |
| Stale forecast | Six hours, the rule the app already uses: no alert fires from an older forecast, and the footer gains an amber dot and "{n} h ago" |
| Darkness over 12 hours | The bezel shows the 12 hours centred on the clear window (or on the middle of darkness when there is no window). From about late October to mid-February at Home darkness exceeds 12 hours |
| Scope | The Tonight popover, the Targets viewability timeline, and the five Targets follow-ons the handover proposes (§5.3). Settings and About keep their current layout and gain only the tokens |

Rulings by the assistant (owner may overrule when reviewing this spec): the dew hint reads "Dew heater advised" rather than the handover's "Heater on", because the app cannot know whether a heater is running; the v0.3 states the mock-ups predate (bright verdict, Clearer sky line, aurora line) sit in one notice row under the bars (§5.1); the warning colour adopts the handover's `status.warning` #F5B041 in place of `Theme.warn` #f4b860, which is visually the same amber and better documented.

## 3. Platform

`glassEffect(_:in:)`, `GlassEffectContainer` and `Glass` are present in the macOS 27 SDK shipped with the Command Line Tools and are marked `@available(macOS 26.0, *)` (checked in the SDK's SwiftUICore interface on 24 September 2026). The package's platform floor stays macOS 14. One view modifier, `nightwatchGlass(in:)`, applies `glassEffect` with `.regular` tinted to `glass.tint` when `#available(macOS 26, *)` and Reduce Transparency is off, and otherwise the solid fill. Groups of tiles and cards sit in one `GlassEffectContainer` under the same check. `glassEffect` is applied after every other appearance modifier, as Apple's guidance asks.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Tonight popover

Top to bottom, as the handover lays it out, with these Nightwatch specifics:

| Component | Content and behaviour |
|---|---|
| Header | "TONIGHT · {SITE}" eyebrow; meta line "{date} · Bortle {n} · EQ tilt {lat}° true north" (+ " · bright night" on a bright plan); settings gear |
| Clock bezel | 60 ticks on a 12-hour face per the handover's tick rule (θ = 6i°, slot = 12i minutes mod 720), coloured by slot: inside the clear window `accent.clear` with glow; dark and under 50% cloud `tick.partcloud`; dark and 50% or more `tick.cloudy`; not dark `tick.daylight`. Hour ticks every fifth. On a bright plan "dark" means nautical darkness. Score numeral and "SKY SCORE" caption inside |
| Headline block | Headline: "Clear window tonight", "Bright night: Moon and planets", the no-window line ("Nothing to see here. Move along." / "No clear window tonight.") or "No astronomical darkness". Then the window time, the bright targets line on a bright plan, and the reason line |
| Reason line | With a window: amber dot + "Held back by {factors}" from §6.1, hidden when nothing limits the score. Without a window: the existing plain reason ("Longest clear run is 2 h from 00:00; the rule needs 3 h."), no dot |
| Clear-sky bars | One column per hour of darkness (nautical on a bright plan), fill = 100 − cloud %, window columns `accent.clear` with glow, others `bar.cloudy`, one peak label over the clearest hour, caption "Clear sky by hour · {source}" |
| Notice row | At most one line, in priority order: aurora ("Aurora: amber (AuroraWatch UK)"), then the Clearer sky line (tappable, lands on the card as in 0.3). Hidden when neither applies |
| Stat tiles | 3 × 2: Dark, Moon, Seeing, Wind, Dew risk, Transparency. Moon tile: 32 pt Moon image, "{n}%" and "Sets {time}" (or "Rises {time}", or "Down tonight"), no label. Dew tile in warning state when risk is High: amber outline and dot, value in text.primary, hint "Dew heater advised" in status.warning. "Frost likely" keeps its current label swap |
| Best tonight | Three cards: thumbnail, catalogue ID, specific type ("Emission nebula", from the catalogue type), "Best {time} · {alt}° up". On a bright plan the Moon and planets. "All targets →" in text.primary |
| Footer | Toggle "Notify at {window start − pre-window minutes}" when a window exists, "Notify when clear" otherwise, bound to the existing notify setting; "Patrol" button; "Updated {time} · {source}", with an amber dot and "{n} h ago" when stale |

### 5.2 Targets viewability timeline

Per the handover's timeline table: a track spanning tonight's clear window on every card (3 pt, `targets.track`), the viewable span lit with a gradient from `accent.clear.low` to `accent.clear` driven by u = (alt − 30)/(alt_peak − 30) clamped to 0…1 sampled at eight or more points, hour ticks, a white best-moment marker, and the caption row "Viewable {start}–{end}" / "Best {time} · {alt}°". With no clear window the track spans darkness instead, shows no lit span, and the caption reads "Not in clear sky tonight"; a target up only outside the window reads "Viewable outside the clear window".

### 5.3 Targets follow-ons (from the handover's proposals)

1. Frame chip: a neutral glass chip "Fills {n}% of frame" replacing the red "Fits frame" badge; hidden when "Fits my field of view" is on.
2. Titles: catalogue ID in text.primary then common name or type in text.secondary, one spacing pattern ("IC 1340"), magnitude on every card ("mag –" when unknown, the handover's "every card or none").
3. Header: a slim clear-sky strip for the window plus a sort control (Best now, Altitude, Size, Brightness) replacing the sort sentence.
4. Glass sidebar and toolbar; the selected row in a white glass highlight instead of system blue.
5. Filters become toggles, with an amber Moon line above them ("Moon 97% · sets 06:10") so "Include Moon-washed" has context; "Near Moon" chip on cards within 15° of a Moon over 50% lit.

## 6. Data additions

### 6.1 Score limiting factors (SkyCore)

`Planner.limitingFactors(plan:) -> [LimitingFactor]` returns, in order of points lost, each score term that lost more than a third of its weight, from the same inputs as `Planner.score`: the Moon term ("a 97% moon"), dew and wind ("high dew risk", "wind at 25 km/h"), seeing and transparency ("poor seeing", "poor transparency"), and cloud ("patchy cloud"). The reason line joins the first two with "and". On a bright plan the Moon is never a factor.

### 6.2 Viewability (SkyCore)

`RankedTarget` gains `viewable: ClearWindow?` (the span inside the ranking window during which the target is above the minimum altitude) and `altitudeSamples: [Double]` (eight or more evenly spaced altitudes across that span), both computed where `track(...)` already samples altitude, and `frameFill: Double?` (target size over the field of view's longer side, 0…1). Existing fields are unchanged; old cached plans fail to decode once and recompute, as in 0.3.

### 6.3 Dew risk (SkyCore)

The popover's dew rule (minimum temperature minus dew point during darkness: under 2 °C High, under 4 °C Medium, else Low) moves into SkyCore as `Planner.dewRisk(_:)` so the tile and the limiting factors share it.

## 7. Accessibility

Re-measure contrast on the live material against light and dark wallpapers; text.secondary must hold 4.5:1 in the worst case. Colour is never the only signal (warnings carry a dot and text, the best marker is a shape). Reduce Transparency uses the solid fill. Charts get sentence labels, for example "Sky score 50 of 100. Clear from midnight to 04:57, clearest hour 00:00 at 89%" and "NGC 6992 Eastern Veil, viewable from 00:00 to 03:28, best at 00:00, 57 degrees up". Tiles and cards grow with Dynamic Type; values never truncate, only common names do.

Measuring (v0.4.0): `swift scripts/contrast.swift` captures the open popover without clicking and samples the bare glass in its side padding. It then prints each text token's worst WCAG 2.1 ratio and fails under 4.5:1. The solid fallback is measured from the tokens themselves:

| Token | On the solid fallback (#14171D) |
|---|---|
| text.primary #E8EAEF | 14.9:1 |
| text.secondary #A8AEBE | 8.1:1 |
| status.warning #F5B041 | 9.5:1 |

Live glass, measured 25 September 2026 on the owner's Mac (macOS 27) from a `screencapture -l` of the open popover:

| Surface | Background | text.primary | text.secondary | status.warning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Popover panel | #292C32 to #2A2D33 | 11.4:1 | 6.2:1 | 7.3:1 |
| Tiles as glass tinted surface.tile (0.4.0 before the fix) | #5A5D63 | 5.4:1 | 2.9:1 (fail) | 3.5:1 (fail) |
| Tiles as a surface.tile fill (0.4.0 as shipped) | #3B3D43 | 8.9:1 | 4.8:1 | 5.7:1 |

So the tiles are not a second glass layer. They are a surface.tile fill on the glass panel, which keeps them near #373A40. The panel figures are stable because the menu-bar popover window draws its own dark material behind the glass, so the wallpaper barely reaches it.
